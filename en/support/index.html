Frequently asked questions

How do I re-grant microphone or speech recognition permission?

Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Himotoku, and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. If you turned a permission off, this is how you turn it back on.

Reminders or Calendar items aren't being added — what do I do?

Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Himotoku, and check the Reminders and Calendar permissions. Items are only added when you tap to confirm.

My entry isn't getting organized — what's wrong?

When the connection is unstable, organizing is held and retried automatically once it's back. Recording and transcription still work offline. If organizing still doesn't arrive, check your Wi-Fi or cellular connection.

How do I export my diary?

Go to Settings and choose "Export (Markdown and audio)". The exported files open in other apps too.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Go to iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions. You can manage or cancel anytime. Cancel during the free trial and you won't be charged.

How do I restore a purchase?

In the app, go to Settings > Plan > Restore Purchase.

Known limitations

  • Requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later (iPad is not supported in v1.0)
  • Supported languages are Japanese and English
  • Speech recognition works best in a quiet place. Background noise can lower accuracy
