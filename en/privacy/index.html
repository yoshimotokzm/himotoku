Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-05 / Last updated: 2026-09-05

Your diary is stored only on this iPhone.

Nowhere else, except a relay used only for AI processing.

What never leaves this iPhone

  • Your voice recordings
  • The full text of your diary (events.jsonl)
  • The search index
  • Files you export

What is sent only during AI processing

  • Organizing: the transcript for that one entry
  • Asking your past: your question and the text of the pages selected to answer it

Destination: Microsoft's Azure OpenAI service (Japan region)

What is never stored

  • Your diary on the developer's servers (there is no database to store it in)
  • Any AI training data (your diary is never used to train AI)

What is kept temporarily

For abuse monitoring, the text that was sent may be retained on Microsoft's side for up to 30 days.

About your voice

Your voice never leaves the device. Speech is transcribed on this iPhone.

Export

Exporting lets you take every entry out as plain text.
